Lydro is an XR-based spatialized sound experience, which utilizes the 3dof head tracking technology available in modern headphone devices. It is a fully interactive experience, where the user can explore the environment and interact with the soundscape.

*The app was conceptualized as part of a larger [research project](https://danishsoundcluster.dk/praksisnaere-loesningsmodeller-til-positiv-og-engagerede-brug-af-musik-og-lydinterventioner-paa-plejehjem/) on Aarhus University in 2023, in collaboration with the VR company [AATE VR](https://aatevr.com/). The whole experience was designed and developed by me.*
