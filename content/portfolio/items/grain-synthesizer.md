This tool includes an audio grain synth generator and a machine-learning-based controller. It is my exploration of grain synthesis and the use of machine learning to create expressive, intuitive controller mappings. These controller mappings are not solely designed to simplify control over an extensive set of parameters, but also expand the synthesizer’s potential and the sound exploration process.
