Lydro

Master Thesis Project

An Interactive 3D Soundscape Experience

Lydro is an XR-based spatialized sound experience, which utilizes the 3dof head tracking technology available in modern headphone devices. It is a fully interactive experience, where the user can explore the environment and interact with the soundscape.

The app was conceptualized as part of a larger research project on Aarhus University in 2023, in collaboration with the VR company AATE VR. The whole experience was designed and developed by me.

Žebra

Sound Design & Scoring

The short fiction Žebra was made in the spring and summer of 2023 and as a key member of the project my responsibilities was everything related to the audio aspects of the production. That includes the audio post-production (dx, sfx design, foley, editing, mixing, etc.) and scoring of the film.

Exploring the film’s sonic universe and mood taught me a lot, and I gained valuable knowledge in audio post-production, particularly in dialogue editing and cleaning up recordings.

The film was screened as part of the official selection at ISFF 2ANNAS 2024 in Riga and the T-Port Lighthouse Selection 2025.
